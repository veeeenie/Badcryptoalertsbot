Tap a coin to see why it does or doesn't have a setup.

Scans the top 100 Hyperliquid perps by 24h volume every hour using daily candles; weekly candles start Monday 00:00 UTC. Every setup is built on a weekly level (the weekly 25/50/100 EMAs or a weekly swing high or low) and only in the direction the weekly trend allows; the entry must be within 10% of price, with reward-to-risk of at least 2.5 for longs and 2 for shorts. The daily chart times the entry: Confirmed means price tested the weekly level in the last three days and closed back on the right side; a daily close well through the level removes the setup. Rating A means another weekly level plus one more level sit within one daily ATR of the entry, B means one other level, C means none. Top setups are the best 8 by rating, confirmation, reward-to-risk and distance. Telegram alerts only top setups, once when price reaches the entry and once when the retest is confirmed, at most 3 alerts per scan. Setups with a stop more than 15% from entry are skipped. Trending means the coin is among CoinGecko's most-searched coins right now. EMAs use Hyperliquid prices, so they can differ slightly from a Binance chart. News and unlocks are refreshed once a day. These are filters for charts worth checking, not trade instructions.
