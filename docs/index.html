Tap a coin to see why it does or doesn't have a setup.

Scans the top 100 Hyperliquid perps by 24h volume every hour on daily candles; weekly candles start Monday 00:00 UTC and EMAs use the same formula as the 3-EMA [Krypt] indicator. Every setup is built on a weekly level (weekly 25/50/100 EMAs or weekly swing highs and lows), in the weekly trend's direction, or against it when the daily 25/50/100 EMAs are fully stacked the other way ("Daily flip"). The first entry must be within 10% of price, blended reward-to-risk above 1.5, the stop within 15% of the average entry, and each tranche order at least $10 at your dollar risk. Confirmed means price traded into the zone and the last daily candle closed back out of it in the trade's direction. If price is already in the zone, or after a strong move, the setup waits for that confirmation. Rating A means another weekly level plus one more level sit at the entry, B means one other level, C none. A coin's weekly EMA is only used once it has that many weekly candles. These are filters for charts worth checking, not trade instructions.
