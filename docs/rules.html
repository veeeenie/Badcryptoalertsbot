Rule Book

The trader's rules, 23 October 2026. New rules get added one at a time, by decision.

  1. The trend is our friend.Trade only in the direction of the weekly trend.
  2. Long timeframes come first.The weekly leads and the daily supports; no dithering on the daily, and nothing below it.
  3. Entry, stop and take-profit all sit at supply and demand regions.Never ad hoc. The stop is backed by that structure and never moved to fit the entry.
  4. Let the trade come to us.Orders wait at the region; we don't chase price.
  5. Don't force the trade.No asymmetric higher-timeframe setup means stay in cash.
  6. Nothing gets in without a stop-loss and a take-profit.
  7. Enter and exit in tranches.
