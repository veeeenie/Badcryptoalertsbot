Rule Book

Reset on 23 October 2026 to the trader's own rules. New rules get added one at a time, by decision.

  1. Long timeframes come first.Weekly and daily structure decide the trade; lower timeframes don't.
  2. The stop is backed by structure (supply or demand) and never moved to fit the entry.
  3. Don't force the trade.No asymmetric higher-timeframe setup means stay in cash.
  4. Nothing gets in without a stop-loss and a take-profit.
  5. Enter and exit in tranches.
