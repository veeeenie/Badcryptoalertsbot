Rule Book

Back to Setup Desk

Trading is a business here, not a guess. Every trade passes all of these or it doesn't get placed. Updated 19 October 2026; changed only by deliberate decision, never mid-trade.

Structure

  1. Higher timeframes first.

    Weekly and daily decide the trade. Ignore every timeframe below the daily.

    setups are built on weekly levels; the daily times the entry
  2. Weekly trend first; a fully flipped daily also counts.

    Trade in the weekly trend's direction. A trade against it is allowed only when the daily 25/50/100 EMAs are fully stacked the other way.

    those setups are labelled "Daily flip" and ranked below weekly-trend setups
  3. A-rated setups only.

    The entry zone is a weekly level with at least one more level sitting at it (another weekly level, or a daily EMA). Setups whose move already played out don't count.

    B/C and played-out setups are removed (backtest: A-only +0.137R a trade, B-rated −0.05R)

Entries

  1. Enter only at the planned zone with resting limits. Never chase.

    If price is already sitting in the zone, enter only after a daily close confirms the rejection. If price leaves without filling, the trade is missed, not chased.

    each setup shows one action: PLACE LIMITS, WAIT FOR CLOSE (a daily candle must close back out of the zone in your direction; daily candles close 5:30 am IST), or ENTER (that close just happened)
  2. Tranches: in and out in at most 3 orders, each at least $10.

    The dollar risk decides how many tranches fit: a tight stop allows 3, a wider stop 2 or 1.

    tranche prices and sizes shown per setup
  3. Against a strong move, wait for a confirmed rejection.

    A strong move is 25% or more in 14 days, or 15% or more from the daily 25 EMA.

Risk

  1. Nothing gets in without a stop and take-profit, placed with the entry.
  2. The stop is backed by structure and never moved to fit.

    It sits beyond the zone and any weekly level just past it. The only move ever made: to breakeven once T1 is hit. A trade that has gone our way is never allowed to turn into a loss.

  3. Only asymmetric trades: more than 1:1.5.

    Blended reward-to-risk from the average entry.

  4. Size by dollar risk, not leverage. Currently $2 per trade.

    That is for the whole trade, all tranches together, not per tranche.

Exits

  1. Enter on the daily, exit at weekly targets.

    Take half at T1 and move the stop to breakeven. Take the rest at the next weekly level (T2). Don't wait for a weekly close to turn against the trade: after breakeven, normal pullbacks stop the runner out first.

    backtest: fixed weekly T2 made +112R (positive 6 of 6 years) vs +42R for exiting the runner on a weekly close

Discipline

  1. Don't force the trade. Cash is a position.
